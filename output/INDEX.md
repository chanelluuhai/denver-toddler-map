# Denver/Arvada Toddler Outing Spots

Updated: 2026-10-01  
Base: Arvada, CO 80004  
Audience: Toddler ~20 months; prefer ~30 min drive

Each pin has a why-note from a named social source. Drive times are rough estimates.

## Apex Center (Indoor Playground) (near)

- **Address:** 13150 W 72nd Ave, Arvada, CO 80005
- **Drive:** ~14 min drive from Arvada 80004 (~4 mi straight-line)
- **Why:** Days.in.denver: 'basically an entire day under one roof' — indoor water park, ice rinks, climbing wall, and a FREE indoor playground for little kids (Colorado-themed with a dedicated tots area for ages ~1–3).
- **Source:** Days.in.denver (@days.in.denver)
- **Hours:** Open daily; check Apex schedule for playground/pool hours
- **Cost:** Daily admission typically ~$10.50 kids / $12.50 adults (playground included; verify current rates)
- **Toddler tip:** Tots section with small spinners/musical boards — ideal for ~20 months. Right in Arvada.
- [Instagram @days.in.denver](https://www.instagram.com/days.in.denver/)
- [Days in Denver Oct newsletter](https://days-in-denver.beehiiv.com/p/days-in-denver-october-newsletter)
- [Apex Center](https://apexprd.org/facility/apex-center/)
- [Clubhouse Adventure Playground](https://apexprd.org/facilities/apex-center-virtual-tour/clubhouse-adventure-playground-and-child-watch/)

## Paco Sanchez Park (near)

- **Address:** 1290 Knox Ct, Denver, CO 80204
- **Drive:** ~19 min drive from Arvada 80004 (~5 mi straight-line)
- **Why:** On Colorado Kids Are Rad’s 10-park list. Huge radio/music-themed adventure playground (giant mic, nets, spiral slide, zipline) — destination playground often called one of Denver’s best.
- **Source:** Colorado Kids Are Rad (@coloradokidsarerad) — 10-park list
- **Hours:** Dawn–dusk
- **Cost:** Free
- **Toddler tip:** Park is HUGE — easy to lose sight of a toddler; go early, stay in toddler-friendly zones, bring a second adult if possible.
- [Instagram @coloradokidsarerad](https://www.instagram.com/coloradokidsarerad/)

## CSU Spur (near)

- **Address:** 4777 National Western Dr, Denver, CO 80216
- **Drive:** ~22 min drive from Arvada 80004 (~6 mi straight-line)
- **Why:** Completely free and open to the public — mock vet clinic, play kitchen, water table, and hands-on spots across three buildings; feels like a children's museum without the price. Open Mon–Fri 9–5 and every 2nd Saturday 10–2 with extra activities.
- **Source:** Days.in.denver (@days.in.denver)
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 9am–5pm; 2nd Saturdays 10am–2pm; closed other Sat & Sun
- **Cost:** Free
- **Toddler tip:** Great for ~20 months: indoor play kitchen, water table, mock vet. Free year-round (except Stock Show grounds fee).
- [Instagram @days.in.denver](https://www.instagram.com/days.in.denver/)
- [CSU Spur (hours/visit)](https://csuspur.org/)
- [Days in Denver newsletter (Oct)](https://days-in-denver.beehiiv.com/p/days-in-denver-october-newsletter)

## Belleview Park (Farm & Train) (FARTHER)

- **Address:** 5001 S Inca Dr, Englewood, CO 80110
- **Drive:** ~47 min drive from Arvada 80004 (~13 mi straight-line) — FARTHER (>30 min; flag)
- **Why:** Free park with playgrounds and creek; seasonal petting farm and miniature train (~$3 ages 2+, under 2 free) — a classic toddler outing highlighted by Colorado Kids Explore.
- **Source:** Colorado Kids Explore (@colorado.kids.explore)
- **Hours:** Park: dawn–dusk. Farm & Train: typically Memorial Day weekend–Labor Day (check city site; 2026 season noted ~9am–3:30pm)
- **Cost:** Park free; farm/train ~$3 each (under 2 free)
- **Toddler tip:** Under-2 free for farm/train. Park playground usable year-round even when farm is closed.
- [Instagram @colorado.kids.explore](https://www.instagram.com/colorado.kids.explore/)
- [Englewood Farm & Train](https://www.englewoodco.gov/parks-recreation-library-golf/play-englewood-recreation/parks/englewood-parks/englewood-farm-train-at-belleview-park)
- [Belleview Park info](https://www.englewoodco.gov/parks-recreation-library-golf/play-englewood-recreation/parks/englewood-parks/find-a-park/belleview-park)

## Cheyenne-Arapaho (Tsistsistas-Hinono'ei) Park (FARTHER)

- **Address:** 9200 E Iowa Ave, Denver, CO 80247
- **Drive:** ~49 min drive from Arvada 80004 (~13 mi straight-line) — FARTHER (>30 min; flag)
- **Why:** On Colorado Kids Are Rad’s 10-park list. Natural-themed playground (bison climber, leaf playhouse, sand play, baby swings) geared toward younger kids (~2–8) plus dog park.
- **Source:** Colorado Kids Are Rad (@coloradokidsarerad) — 10-park list
- **Hours:** Dawn–dusk (typical city/county park)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Toddler tip:** Baby swings and sand play; some structures better for slightly older toddlers — supervise closely.
- [Instagram @coloradokidsarerad](https://www.instagram.com/coloradokidsarerad/)
- [Park background (Arapahoe County)](https://www.arapahoeco.gov/your_county/county_departments/open_spaces/our_work/current_projects/cheyenne_arapaho_park.php)
